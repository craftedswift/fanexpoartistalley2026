All … artists from the official AX 2026 Artist Alley list, ranked by their combined Twitter/X + Instagram follower count.
How it works

Source list: The official AX 2026 Artist Alley page at anime-expo.org/ax2026aalist.

Follower counts: Instagram counts come from each profile's public Open Graph metadata (rounded to the nearest "K" the same way IG displays them — for example 29,341 → 29K). Twitter/X counts come from the public syndication embed endpoint and are exact.

Combined score: score = max(IG followers, X followers). Artists with only one platform score on whichever they have. Artists with neither platform (or with private/empty accounts) score 0 and sort last.

Notes: These counts show where each account stood when this page was built — they don't refresh live. Some accounts are private, suspended, empty, or use uncommon handles, in which case the count shows as "—". Profile pictures from Instagram use temporary signed URLs and may stop loading after a few days; pictures from X are stable.
